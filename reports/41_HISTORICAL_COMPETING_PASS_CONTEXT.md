# Historical competing-pass context: Top4, Meilenweiss and Schneepass

## Evidence recovered

The checksum-pinned Top4 reconstruction remains unchanged: four official regions over eight consecutive seasons from 2017/2018 through 2024/2025. Three official operator PDFs retain the bounded Meilenweiss evidence for 2019/2020, 2020/2021 and 2021/2022. Three additional official PDFs add Schneepass Zentralschweiz evidence. The Brunni 2021/2022 tariff brochure establishes a 14-area product but does not name the full roster, so only its host area is linked. The official 2023/2024 media kit contains 15 area sections, and the official 2024/2025 media kit names the same 15 areas through its media-contact roster.

The combined output contains **92 area-season rows**, **3794 product-listing-season rows** and **103 positive listing-season links**. These reach **23 lift clusters**, including **16 of 104** apparent Path C candidates.

## Winter and summer are not conflated

Each linked area is positive winter evidence. The Meilenweiss annual-pass documents state that the following summer season is included; the 2021/2022 brochure gives an aggregate of 19 winter and 18 summer destinations. Schneepass distinguishes its winter-only pass from SchneepassPlus; the complete 2023/2024 and 2024/2025 sources state 15 winter and 13 summer areas. These aggregate statements do not identify every area-level summer entitlement. Therefore **0 area-specific summer links** are asserted. Product-level summer inclusion is retained separately and never converted into a claim that every winter area operates in summer.

## Conservative scope and negative evidence

Malbun in Liechtenstein is not linked to the supplied Buchserberg-Malbun listing, which is a different Swiss area. Hoher Kasten has no equivalent supplied ski listing. The historical Airolo-Pesciüm label is not widened to Airolo-Lüina, and broad cluster neighbours never inherit a documented listing link. The official Pizol 2018/2019 annual report was checksum-verified, text-searched and visually reviewed but contains no explicit Meilenweiss reference; it is recorded as a non-confirming source and creates neither membership nor absence evidence.

## Decision limit

Three products and fourteen product-seasons still do not reconstruct the full historical multi-resort-pass market. The missing 2022/2023 Schneepass season is not interpolated, non-links do not verify absence, evidence scopes differ by season, several observations postdate Magic Pass treatment anchors, and benefit intensity and prices are not harmonised. Historical-market-absence, pre-treatment, causal, recommendation and composite-score approvals remain false. The result is qualified context, not a ranking input.
