# 02 — Data collection

## Collection scope

Collection was initially limited to high-priority evidence needed for the treatment/outcome feasibility gates. After those gates were assessed, the project added the official HESTA hotel-capacity table, SLF snow history, targeted MeteoSwiss temperature/precipitation, official MeteoSwiss TabsM/RhiresM 1 km monthly grids, the peer-reviewed SPASS OSHD-CLQM 1 km snow-depth grid, STATPOP 2025 municipal population, Eurostat Census Grid 2021, ARE NPVM 2025 transport travel times, current OFT/BAV public-transport stops, and an official SKI/OFT winter-2026 GTFS Static timetable. Employment and commercial datasets remain deferred.

Exact locators and interpretation limits are recorded in `08_MEMBERSHIP_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md`. ## Collection discrepancies and gaps

- The official current page headline refers to more than 100 destinations, while the cached embedded map list contains 95. The mismatch is retained for investigation.
- Twenty-nine membership events have no candidate link to the supplied Swiss listing table; several are foreign destinations, others are composite or differently scoped domains.
- Continuity is complete for the 14 reviewed units, but the broader 88-event historical inventory still contains unresolved destination identities and is not a complete audit of every network destination.
- The exact upstream version and download URL of `bahnen-winter_2056.gpkg` remain unknown.
- SLF IMIS stations support avalanche monitoring and are not direct observations of resort pistes; the 60 low-comparability resort links remain visible and are not feature-ready.
- SwissMetNet stations are regional weather proxies rather than piste measurements. EVO 2016-07 and INT 2013-08 fail the 80% precipitation coverage gate and remain missing; the daily precipitation window is 06:00 UTC to 06:00 UTC the following day.
- TabsM and RhiresM are official spatial analyses derived from MeteoSwiss station observations rather than an independent measurement network. Their 1 km grid spacing is not effective 1 km precipitation resolution, high-altitude interpolation remains uncertain, and precipitation undercatch is material in winter. Axalp and Meiringen-Hasliberg have low station-versus-grid temperature agreement and therefore expose rather than resolve their valley-station representativeness problem.
- SPASS is a modelled, station-informed snow product rather than an independent measurement network. Its OSHD-CLQM field is quantile-mapped to a snow-assimilating OSHD-EKF target, smooths terrain/land-cover effects and inherits precipitation-forcing and SWE-to-depth uncertainty. The distributed `HSCLQMD` variable omits its units attribute; values are explicitly interpreted as metres and converted to centimetres from their physical magnitude and the peer-reviewed HS definition. This inference, and the lack of piste/snowmaking/operations evidence, remain visible limitations.
- The official 2023/24 graubündenCARD operator/transport PDF remains visible in the publisher's search index but returned HTTP 404 during reproducible collection. The audit therefore records exact Splügen-Tambo participation in 2022/23 and leaves anchor-season continuity unresolved.
- NPVM zone polygons do not continuously cover high-alpine terrain. Ninety-eight Swiss listing points therefore use the nearest zone that intersects their independently resolved current municipality; assignment distance is retained (maximum 3.64 km), and the values remain qualified model context rather than live routing.
- The OFT/BAV source includes selected foreign operating points but is not a complete French stop universe, so the three French listing points are left missing. All-mode passenger stops include cableways and chairlifts, and straight-line point distance does not measure routes, timetables, frequency, seasonal operation or validated resort arrival access.
- GTFS Static describes published schedules, not real-time disruption, fares, crowding or guaranteed transfers. All 15 current arrival nodes pass the versioned name/mode/municipality/distance review for descriptive routing, but six are explicitly village/local-centre proxies and door-to-lift walking remains unverified. Official historical archive URLs were identified for all represented exposure winters, but the dynamic archive index disallows the automated collector via `robots.txt`; the 2018–2025 ZIP contents have not yet been checksum-cached or compared.
- The dated competing-pass reconstruction covers five products but still not the full market. It establishes positive presence for conservatively linked Top4, Meilenweiss, Schneepass Zentralschweiz, TopCard and SnowPass Graubünden listings. TopCard core, limited-partner and summer rights remain distinct; SnowPass is winter-only. The official archived Schneepass 2022/2023 winter roster and product-level summer scope are recovered. For SnowPass 2018/2019, an archived official all-canton page supports the 17 major areas named in the industry study and a joint report supports two additional exact hosts. The detailed SnowPass 2018/2019 and 2019/2020 validity PDFs and other regional or reciprocal products remain unavailable, so small-lift completeness and historical market absence are not claimed.
- Eurostat's harmonised grid closes the geographic omission of foreign population from the earlier Swiss-only radius totals, but 13 country-level unallocated records representing 434,787 people across the full European source cannot be placed spatially. Mixed Swiss/foreign cells are not split by country, and the 2021 grid is kept separate from STATPOP 2025 rather than combined across reference years and allocation methods.

Full field-level provenance and plain-language source descriptions are in `metadata/data_sources_master.csv` and `reports/DATA_SOURCES.md`.
